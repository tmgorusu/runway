How the game works

  1. Price and best response (Stackelberg). The utility posts a flexibility price λ. Each feeder zone offers every kW whose marginal wear is at or below λ, up to its hosting limit. Marginal wear folds in enclosure heat, BMS health, cycles, C-rate, and stale telemetry. λ* is the lowest price that covers the call. At λ* no battery can lower its own wear by changing its power, so the dispatch is a Nash equilibrium of the zones' best responses.
  2. Coalition. Calling a zone has a fixed activation cost. Zones are dropped, then added, while wear plus activation plus any shortfall penalty keeps falling. What remains is the targeted subgrid.
  3. Credit (Shapley). The coalition's value is split by each zone's average marginal contribution.
